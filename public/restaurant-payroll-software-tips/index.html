What is actually eating your Monday?

Two different jobs often get mixed together:

  1. Calculating, pooling and paying tips — including a UK tronc where that is how tips are handled — and getting those amounts onto a payslip. That is payroll or tip-management software.
  2. Assembling the weekly picture — sales, labour cost, tip totals and other figures pulled from two tills (POS), rotas and invoices, then typed into a Monday pack. That is a reporting job.

A payroll tool can finish job 1 and still leave job 2 as a manual Monday. A refreshable spreadsheet can finish job 2 and still leave you needing a payroll product to pay people correctly.

When does reporting automation help, and when does it not?

Reporting automation helps when you already have (or can export) the underlying figures — till sales, rota hours, invoice lines, and tip totals from payroll or the till — and someone is retyping them every week. Carlos Alves Consulting connects those exports into one spreadsheet workflow, typically with Excel or Google Sheets and built-in tools such as Power Query, so the weekly report refreshes from your own files.

It does not replace a payroll engine. It will not enrol staff in a pension, submit RTI to HMRC, or legally allocate tips. If the painful part is still “I cannot get two restaurants' tips onto one payroll run”, start with a payroll or tip product, then look at the weekly pack.
