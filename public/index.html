Monday morning doesn’t have to start with copying and pasting

If your weekly numbers live in a till export, a rota file, an inbox of invoices and someone’s memory, the report takes hours — and nobody fully trusts it. Here’s the change I make:

The old way

Retyped, late, and hard to check

  • Sales, labour and costs copied by hand from three or four different exports
  • Invoice errors spotted weeks later — or not at all
  • Every site or room reported in a slightly different format
  • The report is out of date by the time it reaches the owner

With an automated report

Refreshed in under a minute

  • One spreadsheet refreshed from your own exports — zero copying
  • Invoice exceptions flagged the same week, while you can still query them
  • Site-by-site and team-by-team views in one consistent format
  • Formulas unlocked and logic anyone can audit — you own it outright
